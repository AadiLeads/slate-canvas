What's being tested
Baseline — 1024px · quality 0.88 + Opt 1 — max resolution 1536px (tokens same, more detail) + Opt 2 — WebP quality 0.88 → 0.75 (smaller bytes) Opt 1 + Opt 2 — 1536px · quality 0.75
Why Opt 1?  Your 45-run data showed tokens are flat (373–415) across all resolutions — Gemini charges the same regardless. So sending 1536px gives the model more detail at zero extra token cost.
Why Opt 2?  benchmark_4 (dense strokes) hit 108 KB at 1536px. Lower quality reduces upload bytes by ~30–40% with no token impact. We measure if that meaningfully cuts E2E latency.
